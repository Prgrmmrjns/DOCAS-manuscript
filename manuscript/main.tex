\title{{SHAPPROP}: {SCM}-guided synthetic augmentation with composite {TPE} search and {SHAP}-rule feasibility for glycemic tabular models}

\author{First Author}
\author{Second Author}
\address{Institution Placeholder}

\begin{abstract}
\textbf{Introduction:} Gradient-boosted decision trees on observational clinical tables often learn shortcuts that contradict physiology. Post-hoc explainers such as {SHAP} summarise the fitted mapping but do not, by themselves, encode domain structure into training data.

\textbf{Methods:} We implement {SHAPPROP} for free-living {CGM} ({D1NAMO}) in \texttt{scripts/main.py}. A compact list of signed \emph{structural causal model} ({SCM}) rules (\texttt{d1namo.SCM\_RULES}) defines (i) a directed acyclic order over inputs via feature$\rightarrow$feature edges and (ii) optional target-directed edges into \emph{future glucose}. On min--max scaled columns, we fit ordinary least squares ({OLS}) structural modules child$\mid$parents from the current (possibly augmented) training pool, estimate per-child residual scales for Gaussian shocks, and draw synthetic rows in topological order. The synthetic label combines a linear head on the row, an {Optuna}-sampled residual band, and a \emph{rule push} that shifts $y$ along the signed sum of target rules evaluated at the row. An optional insulin \emph{anchor} breaks meal--insulin co-administration confounding when insulin targets glucose. Each {TPE} trial proposes noise, push strength, residuals, and replay parameters, materialises a batch of synthetic rows, retrains default {LightGBM} (squared error, early stopping on real validation rows), and maximises a \emph{composite} score in $[0,1]$ blending validation {RMSE} quality relative to a baseline run with full feasibility weighting. Outer iterations refit the {SCM} on augmented data, append an accepted batch, and stop when neither composite nor feasibility clears a margin. Feasibility is a weighted mean over rules: for target edges we score per-patient {SHAP} values (mass- and count-based agreement with the prior sign); for feature--feature edges we correlate observed parent values with per-row {TreeSHAP} contributions on the child feature (signed {Pearson} vs.\ the rule edge).

\textbf{Results:} We export \texttt{results/d1namo\_shap\_feasibility.json}, an {SCM} graph \texttt{.dot}, and before/after explanation figures; numeric cells in Table~\ref{tab:shapprop_main} should be refreshed after \texttt{python scripts/main.py}.

\textbf{Discussion:} Training stays on supported {LightGBM} losses; priors enter through {SCM}-consistent synthetic rows and a post-fit feasibility functional, which keeps auditing simple but couples scores to the validation sample and rule sparsity.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection*{Motivation and problem setting}
Tabular predictors are routinely deployed in clinical decision support, operational forecasting and cohort analytics \cite{arrieta2020explainable,collins2015tripod}. Labels and inputs are almost always observational, reflecting care pathways, documentation and selection rather than a controlled experiment \cite{prosperi2020causal}. Flexible models therefore learn whatever regularities compress the table, including \emph{shortcuts} that need not survive changes in protocol, device, or population \cite{geirhos2020shortcut}. Standard supervised learning does not, by itself, import the stable structure of physiology or pharmacology.

This matters in two ways. First, \textbf{generalization}: performance degrades when deployment covariates drift from training \cite{prosperi2020causal,carloni2025role}. Second, \textbf{interpretability}: popular post-hoc explainers summarise the fitted mapping, not whether that mapping is compatible with external knowledge \cite{janzing2020feature,doshi2017towards,molnar2022interpretable}. {SHAP} values \cite{lundberg2017unified} are faithful to the model's cooperative-game definition but can still mislead reviewers when the model's partial associations disagree with textbook mechanisms \cite{aas2021explaining}.

\subsection*{Related work}
A long line of work argues that explainability should be grounded in causal or domain structure \cite{heskes2020causal,frye2020asymmetric,carloni2025role,ng2025causalshap}; recent surveys summarise prediction, generation, and explanation on tabular data \cite{fang2024llmsontabular,zhao2024explainabilityllmsurvey}. {SHAPPROP} stays lightweight: no LLM inside the training loop, no custom {LightGBM} objective---only squared error on scaled targets, {SCM}-structured synthetic rows, and {SHAP}-rule feasibility scored after each trial.

\subsection*{Contributions}
\textbf{(1)} An {SCM}-guided sampler that fits linear structural modules from data, injects calibrated shocks in topological order, and applies a signed \emph{rule push} on the synthetic label from target-directed rules. \textbf{(2)} A single-branch {Optuna} {TPE} loop that maximises a bounded composite of validation {RMSE} quality and rule feasibility, with outer iterations that refit the {SCM} on augmented training data. \textbf{(3)} A feasibility functional that mixes per-row {SHAP} agreement for target rules with signed {Pearson} scores on feature--feature edges (parent value vs.\ child {SHAP}), exported alongside graph and explanation diagnostics.

\subsection{Pipeline}
\texttt{scripts/d1namo.py} fixes \texttt{NAME}, \texttt{TASK}, \texttt{TARGET}, \texttt{CSV}, \texttt{FEATURE\_COLUMNS}, and \texttt{SCM\_RULES}. \texttt{scripts/lib.py} supplies graph utilities (\texttt{scm\_topological\_order}, \texttt{scm\_structural\_parents}), {TreeSHAP} main values, signed {Pearson} scoring for feature→feature rules (parent value vs.\ child {SHAP}), and feasibility aggregation. \texttt{scripts/main.py} loads the table, min--max scales inputs and target on the training split, holds out a test fraction, and splits the remaining pool into train vs.\ validation (validation is never synthetically augmented). It fits a baseline regressor, then runs the outer augmentation loop in \S\ref{sec:d1namo_synth}.

\subsection{{D1NAMO}: {SCM}-guided synthetic augmentation}
\label{sec:d1namo_synth}
Let $\mathcal{G}$ be the rule list visualised in Fig.~\ref{fig:d1namo_causal}. Edges with $\mathrm{end}\neq y$ induce structural parents for child features; {Kahn} ordering with a fixed column tie-break yields a generation order. For each child $c$ with parent indices $P(c)$, we fit $\hat x_c = \beta_c^\top x_{P(c)} + b_c$ by {OLS} on the current augmented training matrix $X$ in $[0,1]^d$. Residual standard deviations $\sigma_c$ supply structural noise scales. For a trial we initialise a row, fill exogenous or parentless coordinates by copying stratified real rows, then recursively set
\[
x_c \;\leftarrow\; \mathrm{clip}\bigl(\hat x_c + \nu\,\sigma_c\,z_c,\,0,\,1\bigr),\qquad z_c\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1),
\]
with global shock multiplier $\nu$ proposed by {TPE}. When the rules include insulin$\rightarrow y$, an optional \emph{insulin anchor} replaces $x_{\mathrm{insulin}}$ to mimic counterfactual dosing independent of meal-driven variation. The scaled label is
\[
y \;\leftarrow\; \mathrm{clip}\bigl(h(x) + \delta + \rho\cdot p(x),\,0,\,1\bigr),
\]
where $h$ is an {OLS} linear head $x\mapsto y$ fit on the augmented pool, $\delta$ is a trial-sampled band around the head, $\rho$ is a trial-sampled push magnitude (bounded), and $p(x)=\sum_{(u\rightarrow y)} e_u\,(x_u-\tfrac12)$ sums target-directed rules $(u,y)$ with signed weights $e_u$. Each trial replays its parameters to draw a full batch, concatenates it to training, retrains {LightGBM}, and returns the composite score below. An outer iteration accepts the batch if composite improves against the previous iteration \emph{or} feasibility improves by a fixed margin; then the {SCM} is refit on the enlarged pool.

\subsection{Composite {TPE} objective}
Let $\mathrm{RMSE}_0$ be validation {RMSE} after the baseline fit on real training rows only. For any candidate model with validation {RMSE} $r$ and feasibility $f\in[0,1]$, define {RMSE} channel quality $q_{\mathrm{rmse}}=\mathrm{clip}\bigl(\tfrac12+\tfrac12(\mathrm{RMSE}_0-r)/\mathrm{RMSE}_0,\,0,\,1\bigr)$ and composite $S=(1-w)\,q_{\mathrm{rmse}}+w\,f$ with $w\in[0,1]$ (the implementation defaults to $w{=}1$, emphasising rule alignment while still regularising via $q_{\mathrm{rmse}}$). {TPE} maximises $S$; subsampled rows accelerate feasibility inside trials, while baseline and final reporting use fuller validation statistics.

\subsection{Feasibility scoring}
Each rule carries a weight $w\in[0,1]$ and a sign. For a target-directed rule on feature $u$, we take the vector of per-row {SHAP} contributions $\phi_{i,u}$ and compute a geometric mean of (i) the fraction of absolute {SHAP} mass with $\mathrm{sign}(\phi_{i,u})=\mathrm{sign}(e_u)$ and (ii) the fraction of sufficiently non-zero rows with the same sign agreement. For a structural rule $u\rightarrow v$ (both inputs), we take per-row {TreeSHAP} contributions $\phi_{i,v}$ on feature $v$ and correlate them with observed $x_{i,u}$, mapping the signed {Pearson} $r$ to $[0,1]$ with the rule sign (e.g.\ a negative edge rewards $r<0$: higher $u$ associates with lower attribution on $v$). The overall feasibility is the weighted mean of per-rule scores.

\subsection{Data and domain priors}

\paragraph{{D1NAMO} (regression).} Free-living {CGM} with self-reported meals and insulin; target \emph{future glucose}. After dropping missing rows, $n\approx 6731$ with nine inputs: \texttt{glucose}, \texttt{glucose\_change}, \texttt{time}, macronutrients (\texttt{simple\_sugars}, \texttt{complex\_sugars}, \texttt{proteins}, \texttt{fats}, \texttt{dietary\_fibers}), and \texttt{insulin}. The active \texttt{SCM\_RULES} set in the repository is intentionally small (e.g.\ a dominant insulin$\rightarrow$\emph{future glucose} constraint) so that feasibility focuses on clinically salient directions; additional edges can be re-enabled in \texttt{scripts/d1namo.py}.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol}
Train/test split: 75/25 on the full cohort; within the training pool, 50\% is reserved as validation for early stopping and feasibility (never augmented). {LightGBM} uses fixed shallow defaults with early stopping rounds on validation. We report validation and test {RMSE} in original glucose units, composite score, feasibility, and exported {SHAP} diagnostics.

\section{Discussion}

{SHAPPROP} separates \textbf{(i)}~a sparse signed {SCM} prior, \textbf{(ii)}~data generation that respects fitted linear structure plus optional counterfactual anchors, and \textbf{(iii)}~feasibility as a post-fit functional of {SHAP} values aligned to those rules. Training uses only the standard squared-error booster path. Limitations include sensitivity of {Pearson}-based structural scores to validation size, bias--variance trade-offs when $w{=}1$ on feasibility, and the fact that {OLS} structural modules on observational data need not identify a causal mechanism even when they improve interpretability metrics.
